Chef Mystico

About

A chef who reads minds.

Chef Mystico is a food guessing game with a real prediction engine underneath. It knows 350 dishes from 18 regions of the world, and it plays in both directions: it can find the dish you are thinking of, or hide one and let you find it.

95.9%first guesses right*

* Measured in simulation, for a player who gets one answer in ten wrong. With honest answers it is 100%.

01

Predict My Food

You think of a dish and the chef asks questions. It keeps a probability for every dish in its book and updates them after each answer, so one wrong answer never rules out the right dish for good. Each question is the one expected to teach it the most, and it only names a dish once it is 95% sure.

02

Reverse Clue Mode

The chef hides a dish and describes it one fact at a time. The facts follow that dish’s path through a decision tree, from broad to specific, so each one rules out about half of what is left. The dish’s own hand-written clues open and close the round.

03

The decision tree

Both modes share one table of yes/no questions about the dishes. A scikit-learn decision tree fitted on that table singles out any dish in 8.6 questions on average, against a theoretical minimum of 8.45.

Explore the tree →

04

How it is built

FastAPI and SQLite on the server, NumPy and scikit-learn for the engine, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in the browser. Every game is recorded, so the engine can be checked against how people really play.

Help

How to play.

Two games, one chef. Everything works with a mouse, a finger or the keyboard.

01

Predict My Food

  • Think of a dish from anywhere in the world.
  • Answer each question with Yes, No or Not sure, for the dish as it is usually made.
  • The chef guesses when it is sure. Tell it whether it is right; it gets three guesses.
  • If it fails, type your dish. It will show which of your answers disagreed with its book.

YYesNNoUNot sure

02

Reverse Clue Mode

  • The chef opens with a riddle about a hidden dish.
  • Type a guess at any time. Spelling does not have to be perfect.
  • Stuck? Ask for another hint. After a wrong guess, the next hint shows why it can’t be that dish.
  • Reveal the dish whenever you like. That ends the round with no points.

HAnother hint/Type a guess

03

Scoring

In Reverse Clue Mode each dish is worth 120 points, plus 25 for every difficulty level above the first. Every written clue after the first costs 20, every trait costs 6, and every wrong guess costs 10. A solved dish always earns at least 10. In Predict My Food the scoreboard simply counts who won each round.

04

“The kitchen is dark”

The game server isn’t answering. On the live site it is usually just waking up — the free server sleeps when nobody is playing, and the first visit can take up to a minute. The page picks up by itself.

Running the game on your own computer? Start the server from the food-guess-game folder with

.venv/bin/python -m uvicorn app.main:app

“Not sure” is always safe, by the way: it costs a question but never misleads the chef.

Future scope

What’s next on the menu.

Where Chef Mystico could go from here.

01

Learn from real players

Every answer is already recorded. Fitting the engine’s error model to how people really answer, instead of an assumed one in ten, would keep its confidence honest in live play.

02

Smarter about mistakes

One wrong belief about a dish tends to skew several related answers. Treating related questions as a group would stop the engine counting one misunderstanding several times.

03

Hindi and Hinglish

Questions, hints and reveals in Hindi and Hinglish, so the Jadugar speaks your language.

04

Talk to the chef

Voice answers and questions read aloud, for hands-free play while you cook.

05

A bigger menu

More dishes and regional variants, photographs in the reveal, and filters for diet, region and difficulty.

06

Daily dish

One hidden dish a day for everyone, with scores to compare among friends.
